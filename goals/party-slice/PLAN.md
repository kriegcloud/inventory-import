# Plan

[SPEC](SPEC.md) holds the objective, the open rows O1 to O19 and acceptance. Status is one of `not started`, `doing`,
`done`. Schema and vocabulary come first. Each step says what it builds, what it leaves out and how it is checked
before code is written; it ends with `bun run check` passing, `bun run format -- <its files>`, and one conventional
commit; the app is never broken between steps. Nothing is pushed.

A term enters with what shows it in use in the same commit: every property a mark names, or with `evidence:
"fixture"` a fixture that states it, or gate 5's P11 or guard 3 fails that commit. So steps 9 to 12 each bring one
group of terms with its marks or fixtures and questions, and gates 1 to 8 pass together at the end of each.

## Choices made

Where the sources left a choice open while the goal was specified. Each is Benjamin's to reverse, and step 6 puts
them to him with the open rows.

- **The fixtures' home is the packet**, `explorations/erp-ontology/assets/requirements/cq/`: decision 36 says "in the
  packet", the brief's Map names `explorations/erp-ontology/assets/requirements/` and leaves the home to the slice;
  the `cq/` folder is this goal's. The thread's CQ-P06 fixture stays in `packages/ontology/fixtures/cq/`; moving it
  would change no answer and touch every test that reads it.
- **`Person.role` is lifted with the tenant given beside the rows.** The People page's record has no tenant field and
  the lift needs one; the page lists one organization's members, so the caller names it. Undoing it changes one
  parameter of the lift's test.
- **A decision's fixture keeps its name as text.** "test_Ana Ruiz" becomes an IRI without the space, since the lift's
  IRI segments admit no space.
- **No Sutton data is read.** Decision 123 forbids writing a record of the demo workspace here; it does not forbid
  reading one. Decision 36 proves the model on fixtures, and the workspace holds no company, contact or item.
- **The coverage table's "No term" column** follows report 17's readers where they gave a verdict and decision 128
  for settings and layouts, with one departure: the readers say `UserProfile`'s names, job title, image and about
  "describe the Person", and the table puts them under "Waits on" (O4, O5) with the person, not under "No term";
  `Contact`'s names and job title, on which the readers were silent, go with them, and `phoneExt` and
  `phoneNumberExt` go with the phones (O10). Where the readers were silent the table proposes: `Contact.notes` and
  `dateCreated`; `Organization.status`, `accessExpiresDate`, `domain`, `logo`, `replyToEmail`,
  `orderRequestInstructions` and `shippingPreventOverPicking`; `User.username`; and `CurrentUser.canManageRoles`
  (no term). `hasAccountingIntegration` and `hasActiveAccountingIntegration` go to slice 8 with
  `activeAccountingIntegration`, the integration links of decision 128. O14 puts the whole column to Benjamin. The
  Sutton settings that are business rules (`moduleSettings`, one of report 17's eleven, the number prefixes, the
  alert settings, `shippingPreventOverPicking`) are written later as rules of the terms they govern, as decision
  128 says.
