The screen after Confirm is the one the person keeps. It says what happened to every row of the file, counted from
what was stored, not from what was meant to be.

- **Imported, changed and left out, each said.** The result names what came in, what was changed on the way in (a
  unit, a reading, a pick, and who made it) and what was left out. Check: on the sample, each of the three has its
  line, or says "none".
- **What was left out comes in named parts**, each with its count and its reason: headings apart from rows that wait
  on the person, shipped apart from left out. Never one lump ("30 not imported"). Check: no count of rows not
  imported stands without its parts beside it.
- **The parts add up to the rows in the file**, for each kind of record the task names (orders and their lines), and
  the screen shows that sum. Check: add them on the screenshot.
- **Every number is read back from what is stored**, not from the file or the review. Check: each count on the
  result equals a count of the stored records.

## Also

- **A second load of the same file says what is already there**, counted from the stored records, never from the
  file's rows that match one. Check: load the sample twice; "already there" equals the stored count, and nothing new
  is stored.
- **The tester's check**: on a copy with nothing imported yet, press nothing but Confirm, then write three numbers
  side by side: rows in the file, what the result says, what is stored. Then again with every question answered. A
  difference is a finding. Check: the `qa` report has both rows of three.
