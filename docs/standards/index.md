# Read before building

* [Someone new can start](first-screen.md) - A first-time user can say what the screen is for and reaches the review in one click.
* [One screen, few words](review-screen.md) - A review screen fits 1440 x 900 with no page scroll, at most 40 words outside the table, one filled button.
* [The app shows it is working](working.md) - Nothing blocks the screen, every press shows its result or its progress at once, a large file never freezes the page, and data already fetched shows without a wait.
* [Leaving is safe](saved-progress.md) - A hard refresh, a closed tab or a mis-click never makes the person repeat work, and they resume where they stopped.
* [Every step can be undone](undo.md) - Every decision can be undone until Confirm, Confirm says what it will do, and a confirmed import can be taken back while its records are unchanged.
* [An error says what to do next](errors.md) - An error says what went wrong and what to do instead, in the person's words, every problem at once, and never shows technical detail.
* [The result tells the whole truth](after-confirm.md) - After Confirm, the screen says what was imported, changed and left out, in named parts that add up to the file, each number read from what is stored.
* [The app proposes, the person decides](proposals.md) - No value changes silently; changes and agent actions are shown first, and the person accepts them.
* [Agent decisions are structured and attributed](agent-decisions.md) - Agent decisions are structured outputs with a confidence level and its basis, and who decided is recorded.
* [How I work](working-method.md) - Design order, pushback, plain before ambitious, measured numbers, and how agents share work.
* [Say only what was checked](honest-reporting.md) - Claims name their check, decisions name who made them, and trust fields are honest.
* [Handing work to me](handing-over.md) - Put working pieces where I can try them and read the code, say exactly what was looked at, and keep open questions, defaults and findings in front of me.
