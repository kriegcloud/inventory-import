# The map

One row per page of [concepts/](concepts/). The verdict is the page's own, in one of three forms; where the brief
recommends otherwise, the row says so and names the decision:

- **Use**: it works on the published release, `2.0.0-beta.87`.
- **Use, from main**: it exists only on the library's `main` (read at `dbfd0ddb`), not on npm. How the app takes what is only on `main` is a decision for the brief.
- **Cannot yet**: neither release gives it, or this app's deployment cannot run it; the page says what is in the way and gives the nearest thing that works.

A page that covers several arrangements has a row for each verdict it gives. Nothing here is used by the app today: no file imports the library. "Today" says what the app has in its place.

54 pages; verdicts given: 46 Use, 8 Use from main, 4 Cannot yet, and 1 put to Benjamin as decision 28 of the brief, answered on 2026-11-22 (singletons and crons).
